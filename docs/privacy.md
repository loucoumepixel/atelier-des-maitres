# Privacy policy — Atelier des Maîtres

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

Atelier des Maîtres is a paint-by-numbers app. It runs entirely on your phone.

**No personal data is collected.** The app has no account, shows no ads, contains no analytics and sends nothing over the Internet. It does not even request permission to access the Internet.

**Imported images.** When you import an image, Android lets you pick it in the system photo picker: the app only receives the image you chose, never access to your gallery. A reduced copy is kept in the app's private storage so you can paint it. It is never transmitted.

**Sharing.** The "Share the canvas" button opens Android's share menu with the image of your canvas. You choose the receiving app yourself; Atelier des Maîtres transmits nothing on its own.

**Progress.** Your canvases in progress are stored only on your phone. They may be included in your device's Android backup if you have enabled it, according to your Google account settings.

**Deletion.** Removing an imported image (from its card in the gallery) deletes its copy and its progress. Uninstalling the app erases all its data.

**Contact.** For any question, open an issue at https://github.com/loucoumepixel/atelier-des-maitres/issues
